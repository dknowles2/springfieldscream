For the grown-ups

How scary is it, honestly?

Think Disney's Haunted Mansion at one one-thousandth the budget. Atmosphere does the heavy lifting: fog, low light, spooky music, a few animated props.

Most kids around 6 and up have a great time. Younger than that, walk in holding a hand and you'll be fine — and if anyone wants out early, just say so and we'll flip the lights on and open the side gate. No questions, no judgment.
